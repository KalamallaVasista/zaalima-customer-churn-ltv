# Metabase Dashboard Guide

## Dashboard Objective

The Metabase dashboard provides a visual overview of customer churn, churn risk, customer lifetime value, and retention priorities.

## Customer Churn Overview

This section focuses on historical customer churn patterns.

Key metrics and analysis include:

- Total Customers
- Churned Customers
- Overall Churn Rate
- Average Monthly Charges
- Churn by Contract
- Churn by Payment Method
- Churn by Internet Service
- Churn by Online Security
- Churn by Tech Support

## Churn Risk

This section focuses on model-generated churn risk.

Customers are grouped into:

- High Risk
- Medium Risk
- Low Risk

Churn probability is used to identify customers who may require retention attention.

## LTV and Retention

Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) is combined with churn risk to support retention prioritization.

Retention groups:

- High Priority
- Medium Priority
- Low Priority

High-priority customers represent customers who should receive greater attention based on their churn risk and customer value.

## Dashboard Purpose

The dashboard helps present the project in three stages:

1. Understand historical churn.
2. Identify customers at risk of churn.
3. Prioritize valuable customers for retention.

This supports data-driven customer retention decisions.
